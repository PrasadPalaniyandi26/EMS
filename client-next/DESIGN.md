# NTEMS design language

One set of rules for every page and dialog. Tokens live in `src/app/globals.css`, the typeface in `src/app/layout.tsx`. `npm run check:design` fails the build of a change that steps outside them.

## Principles
- Quiet ledger: two solid surfaces (canvas and surface), hairline borders, one accent. No gradients, glow, blur or shadows except menus and dialogs.
- Content is the design: plain-text table cells, right-aligned tabular amounts.
- Sentence-case text everywhere. No uppercase or letter-spaced labels.
- Same job, same component, same place.

## Tokens (IBM Carbon Gray 90 dark / Carbon White light)
- Surfaces: `--shell-bg` (canvas: sidebar, top bar), `--shell-surface` (content, panels), `--popover` (menus, dialogs), `--table-header`, `--shell-hover`.
- Text: `--shell-text`, `--shell-text-muted`. Borders: `--shell-border` (hairline), `--input` (control boundary, 3:1).
- Accent: `--shell-accent` (+ `--shell-accent-fg`). Status: `--success --warning --info --danger`, workflow `--status-*`, `--role-*`, `--nav-*`.
- Never write a hex colour, `rgb()` or a raw palette class (`text-blue-500`) in a component.

## Type, weight, shape
- Typeface: IBM Plex Sans only. Components never set a font family.
- Sizes (px): 12 secondary and labels, 13 body and table cells, 14 inputs and subtitles, 15 panel and dialog titles, 16/18 figures, 22/26/28 page and brand.
- Weights: 400, 500 (names, emphasis), 600 (headings, figures). No bold, extrabold or black. Only 400, 500 and 600 are loaded, so nothing heavier can render.
- **Roles on a dashboard or panel list** (so sections scan by role, not by reading): panel title 15/600; lead figure 28/600 with -0.01em tracking and tabular figures; supporting figure 18/600; list text 13/400; the figure in a row 13/600 with tabular figures; secondary text 12/400 muted; a status word beside a figure 13/500 in its status colour. Sections are 24px apart and panels inside a section 24px apart, so each block reads as its own group.
- **Title Case for names that label a place or a column:** menu names (sidebar, bottom nav), the page title in the top bar, dialog titles and table headers start every word with a capital (`Product Costing`, `Price And Costing For BO-0058`, `Promised Delivery`). Write them in sentence case in code and let the shared styling do it: `capitalize` is set once on the sidebar, bottom nav, top bar title, `ModuleModal` title and in `globals.css` for every `th`. Do not add your own `uppercase` or `lowercase`, and do not retype strings in Title Case. Everything else (buttons, field labels, hints, panel headings, tabs, messages) stays in sentence case.
- Radius: `rounded-md` (4px) small elements and controls, `rounded-lg` (6px) containers, panels and dialogs, `rounded-full` for dots and switches only. Buttons are rectangular, never pills.

## Page anatomy
- `ModuleShell`: toolbar row (48px) then the table fills the area; the detail panel docks on the right with a left border. No gaps, no floating cards.
- Dashboard-style pages: `StatStrip` of `StatCard` cells, then bordered panels.
- **The Home dashboard leads with three figures, not six.** One bordered band of hairline-divided cells: what needs the user (Pending approvals), how the budget stands (Budget used, with a bar and a word), and the Net balance with its parts (Income, Expenses, Transactions) beside it. Lead figures are 28px/600 and supporting figures 18px; no icon tiles. Panels follow in order of use: trend and budgets, activity and approvals, breakdowns last. Lists inside a panel are hairline-divided rows, never boxed rows.
- **A dashboard says something specific, never something generic.** No greeting such as "Welcome back, admin": the header subtitle names the period the figures cover. A net balance is shown as income less expenses, the way it is worked out. "Where does it go" breakdowns are ranked lists (name, amount, share, bar), not donuts with a default palette. An empty panel says what is missing and what to do about it, in the product's words ("No active budget plan. Create one in the Planner…"). Shortcuts that only repeat the menu are left out; the page keeps the actions that add a record.
- **Analysis pages use the same parts as the Home page.** `Panel` and `PanelLink` (`components/shared/panel.tsx`) for every titled block and `PeriodBar` (`components/shared/period-bar.tsx`) for the period; do not rebuild them. Panel titles carry no icon and are sentence case. The widest content goes full width (a heatmap is never squeezed into half a row), and spend against budget is shown as a row with the amounts, the share and a bar, with no bar at all when no budget is set.
- **Colour on a dashboard is a role, not decoration.** Income uses `--success` and spending uses `--chart-1` in every figure and chart, so a figure and its series match. `--danger` and `--warning` appear only for something wrong or near a limit (over budget, a negative balance), always with a word beside the colour. Routine expenses are never red.
- Tables: `DataTable` + the cells in `components/data-table/cells.tsx` (`TextCell`, `IdCell`, `DateCell`, `MoneyCell`, `WholeCell` for whole-rupee planning amounts, `NumCell` for any right-aligned number, `StatusText`, `ToneText`). Every table in the app is a `DataTable`, in a dialog as well as on a page; never a raw `<table>` and never a local cell component. Status is coloured plain text, never a pill, dot or avatar. Page size fits the screen (`useFitPageSize`); first load shows skeleton rows. The Previous and Next buttons appear only when there is more than one page. Server-paged lists pass `manualPagination` and `totalRows`; a report passes `footerRow` for its totals.
- Money: `lib/money.ts` (`money`, `plain`, `unitPrice`, `unitPlain`) for planning and costing figures, `formatCurrency` for ledger amounts. Do not write another `Intl.NumberFormat` in a page.
- **One record, three places.** A project, product or order lives in Manage (its facts), Costing (its cost and margin) and the Planner (its budget). Each page links to the other two for the same record: Manage rows have **Open costing**; the costing and Planner pages carry **Open in Manage**, **Open costing** and **Budget** links at the right end of the tab row (`TabBar trailing`, `RecordLink`); a Manage list opens with the record selected when the address has `?id=`. Add, Edit and Delete in Manage show only to the roles the API lets change them (managers and admins; orders also Finance and Operations).
- The budget pages group by what they answer: **Planner** (the plan, by project, product, order and overhead) and **Monitor** (Lines, Alerts, Changes, Month close). A page that belongs with another becomes a tab there and its old address redirects.
- Toolbar: search and filters, `Add`, and the actions for the selected item (`ToolbarActions`).

## Standard page template (approved)
Every list or workspace page is built in this order, top to bottom. The reference is `budget/products/[id]/page.tsx`.
1. **Title** in the top bar (`useHeaderStore.setHeader`).
2. **Tabs** (`TabBar`) when a page has several views. They are the first row under the title.
3. **Action row** (`DataTableToolbar` in the `ModuleShell` `searchRow`). Left: the actions that are always available (`Add …`, `Propose …`), then the actions for the selected row (`Edit`, `Delete`, `Approve` …) from the same `DetailActionDef[]` the panel uses. Right, always in this order: **Import, Export, Filters, Search**. Import and Export are the `onImport` / `onExport` props of `DataTableToolbar`, never entries in the left action list; filters are the `filterDefs` prop (rule 8).
4. **Work area**: the `DataTable` fills the space. The right panel is fixed beside it.
5. **Right panel** (`ModuleIntelligencePanel`): nothing selected shows a summary of the whole record (`idle`), a selected row shows its key facts (`DetailSummary`, `DetailSection`, `DetailGrid`), and the row's actions are pinned at the bottom (`DetailActions`, at most three).
6. **Every action button has an icon, and no two actions on a page share an icon colour.** Icons are lucide, coloured with a status token: Add green (`--success`), Edit blue (`--info`), Delete red (`--danger`), and amber (`--warning`), purple (`--shell-accent`) for the rest. Because Delete is red, no other action on that page is red. `ToolbarActions` looks the icon up by label in `TOOLBAR_ICONS`; any other action passes `icon` itself. A missing icon logs a warning in development.
7. **No actions inside rows** (no pencil or bin icons) and **no in-place editing** in a table or grid. A cell is read-only text. Every change goes through Edit (or Add) into a `ModuleModal` dialog, so several values can be changed in one save.
8. **Filters: up to two sit in the row, more go into one dialog.** A screen declares its filters as `FilterDef[]` (`components/data-table/filters.tsx`) and passes them as `filterDefs`. With one or two, they show as dropdowns before Search. With **three or more**, the row shows a single `Filters…` button (with the number of active filters) that opens a dialog: every filter is a labelled field, the list changes as you choose, `Clear all` resets them and `Done` closes it. Never put three dropdowns in the row, and never hand-build filter controls on a page. A filter that needs more input, such as a custom date range, adds it with `extra`. A yes/no filter uses the plain values `All`, `Yes` and `No` with `placeholder` set to the filter's name: the same native dropdown as the others reads `Product` until a value is chosen, and its list holds only the plain values; do not write "Product: yes" into the options. `Filters…` is a control like the dropdowns, so its icon is neutral and is not counted among the action colours.
9. **A list that leads to a detail page selects first, then opens.** Clicking a row only selects it: the right panel shows its key facts and an **Open** action (`DetailActionDef`, `variant: 'primary'`, `FolderOpen` icon) in both the toolbar and the panel footer takes you to the detail page. A row click never calls `router.push`. This holds for every landing list (Product costing, Project costing, Order costing and any new one). The reference is `budget/order-costing/page.tsx`; `npm run check:design` fails on `onRowClick` with `router.push`.
10. **The panel's idle state and main action follow one pattern.** Idle (nothing selected): a `DetailSummary` with a headline figure, then one `DetailSection` holding a small `DetailGrid` of counts, and at most one short sentence saying where the rest lives. No step-by-step instructions; the Open button makes the next step obvious. With a row selected, the first action is the one the record needs next (Start build, Deliver, Approve), then **Open**, then the rest. On a page that has no such next step (the costing landings), **Open** is the main action. A page whose actions moved to another page says so in one line in the panel.

## Sidebar and menu (`components/layout/sidebar.tsx`)
- **Business holds one page per object** (Clients, Projects, Products, Orders). The list is the master record and its costing in one: nothing selected shows only Add; a selected row shows Open and the steps valid for where it stands. Old Manage and Costing addresses redirect here.
- **Group order follows the work:** Home, Transactions, Business, Costing Data, Budget, Analytics, System. The records people enter and maintain come first, then the costing built from them, then the budget that watches it; settings (Organization, Budget settings, Audit Log, Settings) last. A new page goes into the group that matches what it does; add a group only when none fits. A group with no items does not exist.
- **Names:** Business lists are plural nouns (Clients, Projects, Products, Orders); a list shows the record and its costing together. Two menu items never share a name.
- **Groups collapse.** The header is a button with a chevron (`aria-expanded`); the open or closed state is saved per person (`groups` in the sidebar store). A group is open by default when it holds the current page, plus Transactions. Opening a page opens its group. The collapsed slim sidebar shows every icon with group dividers.
- **Items:** 14px, weight 400, row about 36px; the active item is weight 600 in the accent colour on an accent tint (`.nav-active`) with `aria-current="page"`; there is no side stripe, because a coloured edge on a list item is a template mark and the tint, weight and colour already say where you are. Group headers are 12px, weight 500, muted. Icons are 16px at 75% until hovered or active, in **one `--nav-*` colour per group** (Home blue, Transactions emerald, Budget cyan, Costing orange, Costing Data amber, Analytics purple, Manage indigo, System slate), so a colour tells you where you are and items in a group never differ for no reason. A new page takes its group's colour; a new group takes an unused one.
- **The collapse control** sits at the top, beside the brand, not at the bottom.
- **One left edge and one gutter.** In the sidebar the brand text, the group labels and the icons all start at 24px. The top bar title, the footer text and the page content all start 16px in from the sidebar (`1rem`, set once on `.fixed-top` and `.footer-fixed`), and the right-hand controls end on the same gutter, so the title sits directly above the content it names. The title and subtitle share a baseline, and a long title truncates before it can push the controls. The list of pages scrolls inside the sidebar with a thin visible scrollbar, and the phone backdrop sits above the top bar and bottom bar (`z-[45]`) so it dims them too.
- **The brand is a link to Home.** Navigation landmarks are labelled (`Main navigation`, `Pages`, `Quick pages`), every control shows a keyboard focus ring (never `outline-none` without a replacement), and the phone slide-over closes with Escape. Open alerts show as a count beside the name, and as a dot on the icon when the sidebar is slim.
- **Menus and the notification list are plain.** Menu items are sentence case ("Switch role", "Sign out"). Notifications are rows you can reach by keyboard: a small type icon, the title (bold while unread), the message and the time; no tinted circles, no "N new" pill, no cute empty-state line ("No notifications."), and no arrow added to a link.

## Detail panel (`components/shared/detail/detail-kit.tsx`)
`DetailSummary` (title, reference, status, headline figure), `DetailSection` (sentence-case heading, optional text action), `DetailGrid` + `DetailField` tiles two per row, `DetailAttachment`, `DetailTimeline`, `DetailAllocation`. The item's actions (`DetailActionDef[]`, built once per page, empty when nothing is selected) render both in the toolbar and pinned at the bottom of the panel with `DetailActions` (max three: filled main, outlined secondary, red-outline destructive).

## Dialogs and forms
- Every dialog is `ModuleModal` (or `ConfirmDialog` for confirmations): one surface for header, body and footer, 15px Title Case title (set by `ModuleModal`), footer Cancel then the main action. Never a raw `Dialog`.
- Fields use `Field` (label linked to the control, hint and error) with `inp` / `NativeSelect` / `CurrencyInput` / `PhoneInput` / `EmailInput`: transparent fill, 3:1 border, visible focus ring. Same on pages and in dialogs.
- Required fields are marked with the red asterisk (`Field required`). A dialog never lets you save while one is empty or invalid: pass `canSave` to `ModuleModal` (the save button stays disabled), and for custom footers disable the main button the same way. No "choose X first" hints; a dependent dropdown is simply disabled until its parent is chosen. `check:design` fails a dialog that has required fields but no `canSave`.
- A dialog with more than five fields uses `size="lg"` and **two columns** (`md:grid-cols-2`), grouped by meaning: what the record is on the left, how it is costed, dated or limited on the right, with any total or result last. Never three columns, and never `xl` for a form. Fewer fields stay single column at `md`. Reference: the estimate line dialog in `planning-modals.tsx`.
- Dates in hints and tables are shown as dd/mm/yyyy with `formatDate`; a native date input is paired with a hint showing the chosen date in that format.
- Say **Order**, not "build order", in anything the user reads: menus, titles, labels, toasts and hints. (Orders live in one place: Business > Orders.) `npm run check:design` flags "build order" in screen text.
- Toggles that pick one option are `SegmentedControl` (a labelled radio group). Approve/reject/void/send-back dialogs are `DecisionModal`; receipt upload is `FileDropzone`; GST rate and inclusion are `GstRateSelect` / `GstIncludedSwitch`. Reuse these; don't copy their markup.

## Accessibility floor
Text 4.5:1, control boundaries 3:1, focus ring on every control, every control labelled, no horizontal scroll at 375px, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, theme stored as `{"state":{"theme":"light|dark"}}` in `localStorage['ems-theme']`.

## Related-page shortcuts
- Every page that has companions shows text links to them **at the right end of the tab row**, in the accent colour at 13px. A tabbed page gets them from `TabBar` automatically. A page with no tabs of its own is wrapped in `PageFrame` (`components/shared/module/page-frame.tsx`), which draws the same row with the page name as its one tab. A page with no links has no row.
- The list lives in one place, `src/lib/related-pages.ts`, one rule per page. **Add a page's links there, never inside the page.** A link to the page you are on, or to one the role cannot open, is left out.
- Keep it to two or three links: the budget plan of the same record, the list it came from, or the next page in the same job (Expense to Approvals, Monitor to Planner). A detail page links back to its list with `?id=` so the record stays selected.
- Do not put them in the top bar or the toolbar; the toolbar is for actions on the selected row. Phone width is not settled yet.

## Home
- Home is the business overview, loaded from one call (`/dashboard/overview`) and refreshed every minute and when the tab regains focus. It covers the records the role may see: everyone for Admin and Finance, the team for a Manager, one's own for the rest (the header says which).
- Order of the page: period bar and "Updated" time; five headline figures in one strip (Net result, Income, Expenses, Awaiting approval, Budget used or Claims to reimburse), each with the change against the period before and, for the first three, the last twelve months as a line; the monthly chart beside **Needs attention**; Spend by category, Budget health and Projects; Order book and Recent activity.
- Needs attention is a worst-first list of things to act on, each a link to the page that deals with it. Add an item in `DashboardOverviewService.attention`, not on the page.
- A month in the chart is clickable and sets the period to that month. Budget and the portfolio are for the roles that can open those pages; others see Spend by category and Recent activity.
- Roles that can see invoices (Admin, Finance, Manager, Operations) also get a **Clients owe you** figure in the strip and a **Receivables** panel beside the Order book (outstanding, overdue, drafts, and the clients who owe most). Overdue invoices, drafts and POs ending within 14 days join Needs attention.

## Clients: purchase orders and invoices
- Clients has three tabs for the roles that can see billing (Admin, Finance, Manager, Operations): **Clients**, **Purchase orders**, **Invoices**. The Clients list gains Outstanding and Overdue columns, and the panel a Billing section.
- An invoice is a **draft** until Finance or Admin issues it. Issuing gives it the next number (`NTIN-YYMM-NNNNN`, from the invoice date, one running sequence that starts at 10001 and never resets) and fixes the seller, the buyer and the tax. After that it is cancelled with a reason, never edited or deleted.
- Tax follows the states: CGST and SGST when the company and the client are in the same state, IGST otherwise. The state comes from the GSTIN, or from the state named on the client.
- A payment becomes one approved Income entry for the amount received. The TDS a client deducts counts towards the invoice but is not income. Income made this way is changed on the invoice, not on the Income page.
- The printed invoice is paper: dark ink on white in every theme. Its colours live in `lib/invoice-sheet-css.ts`, outside the design check, on purpose.
- Company details (legal name, GSTIN, state, bank, signatory) are on **Organization > Company**.
- Projects and orders show a **Billing** section (`BillingSection`, in the list panel and on the Open page): the contract or order value, the client's POs, what is invoiced against it (taxable value, as a share and a bar), received, outstanding and overdue, and links to the invoices. An invoice counts when it names the project or order, or sits on a PO that does.
- A PO can cover a project, an order, or both. Reports (Admin and Finance): **Receivables Ageing** (what each client owes, by days past due) and **GST Sales Register** (issued invoices by invoice date, with the tax split; a cancelled invoice stays listed at no value so the number series has no gaps).
